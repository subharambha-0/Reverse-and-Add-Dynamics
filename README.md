Reverse-and-Add Dynamics

A Carry-Based Mathematical Investigation

Author: Subharambha Lamsal
Field: Number Theory
Research status: Independent investigation

Overview

This project investigates the mathematical structure of reverse-and-add transformations, with particular attention to the behaviour of the integer 196.

The research examines relationships between decimal digit symmetry, carry propagation, digit sums, modular arithmetic and palindrome formation.

Rather than treating reverse-and-add sequences solely as computational processes, this investigation studies their underlying arithmetic properties.

Principal Results

The investigation develops the following mathematical results:

1. Carry-based digit-sum identity

For a positive integer (n),

$$
R(n)\equiv (-1)^{L-1}n\pmod{11}
$$

where (S(n)) is the decimal digit sum and (C(n)) is the total number of carry-outs during the addition.

2. Modular recurrence

For the reverse-and-add sequence,

[
n_{t+1}=n_t+R(n_t),
]

we establish

[
n_t\equiv 2^tn_0\pmod{11}.
]

3. Decimal structural analysis

The investigation represents reverse-and-add transformations using mirrored digit-pair sums and carry propagation.

Research Objectives

* Understand the relationship between digit symmetry and carries.
* Derive mathematical restrictions on palindrome formation.
* Investigate modular properties of reverse-and-add sequences.
* Examine whether these properties can contribute to understanding the integer 196.
